ABOUT THE MACHINE

Not an archive.
A way inside.

REWINDYR-ERA is an interactive time machine for connected technology and the internet, from the ideas of 1960 to the possibilities of 2026.

The goal is to experience an era through original fictional interfaces, communities, and digital worlds—not just read about it.

This first version contains the home-page controls and era introductions. The simulated internet will be built in later tabs.
